# Tiny Harvest direction

The previous garden delivery was rejected by the user. This replacement uses free third-party assets only. No image generation, synthesized artwork, or synthesized audio is part of this revision.

Use Kenney's Tiny Farm pack for every illustrated world object and match token. Preserve its warm, dark-outlined, 16-pixel art, green foliage, terracotta buildings, ochre soil and crop colors. Render imported sprites with nearest-neighbor sampling. Keep each world object replaceable in the native editor. UI uses cream text, a quiet dark wood frame, and simple square corners from the same palette. Avoid mixing painterly scenes, glossy gems, unrelated icon packs and generic success cards.

The farm is one continuous top-down space. A path links an empty barn site, a neglected orchard and bare beds. Successful matches restore the orchard, grow the crops, then rebuild the barn. The camera and positions stay consistent. Matched produce travels toward the farm before the world changes, making the reward causal. The full farm remains visible through the CTA.

The lower half is a five-by-five crop board. Swipes and two taps must both work. Tiles move to their destinations, wrong swaps reverse, matches pop, and remaining crops fall into the gaps. Do not replace the board after every move or ask the player to press a separate generic repair button. Quiet board surfaces let the silhouettes and colors carry recognition.

Source family plan:

- World, crop tokens and farmer: raw Kenney Tiny Farm PNGs, CC0. One pack gives consistent outlines, scale and perspective. Original source archive and license retained.
- Sound effects and completion: Kenney Interface Sounds and Music Jingles, CC0. Convert licensed OGG audio to bounded PCM WAV without composing new content.
- Music: a short licensed chiptune excerpt appropriate to the pixel world. Source and exact conversion recorded before use.
- Layout, hit regions, timing and transitions: native scene objects and code. These remain editable and do not substitute procedural hero artwork for the source sprites.
- Typography: the native runtime's built-in 5×7 bitmap font, with dark-wood/cream plaques and hard-edged button frames. Dynamic counters and messages remain editable text; no browser font download or baked text artwork is used.
- Feedback: nearest-neighbor crop motion, restoration bounce, square pixel confetti and short bounded excerpts from the licensed cues. Music stays below the match, repair and completion sounds; rejection is quieter and every reward remains legible when muted.

Review opening, a wrong swap, the first match and restoration, continued play, final transformation, CTA, replay and rotation. Judge controls and motion on the exported playable. Automated assertions establish behavior only. The user's creative acceptance remains pending.
